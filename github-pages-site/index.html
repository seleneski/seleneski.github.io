Merhaba! I am Selen Eski, third-year PhD student in Finance at Bocconi University.

I hold a B.Sc. in Business Administration from Middle East Technical University (Ankara, Turkiye) and an M.A. in Management from Boğaziçi University (Istanbul, Turkiye). Before starting my academic journey, I was an FX trader in the Treasury Division of Türkiye İş Bankası.

My research interests focus on Financial Macroeconomics, Financial Markets, and Institutions, with a particular emphasis on emerging markets.
